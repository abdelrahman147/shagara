# shagara Irrigation Playbook
## Drip schedule
For a 20 litre container, start with 600 to 800 ml every morning. Increase the volume by 20 percent during heatwaves and reduce it after cloudy days. Always check the top 2 cm of soil before repeating a cycle. Automated digital timers should trigger drip emitters at 6:00 AM before direct rooftop solar radiation heats the surface.

## Water quality
If tap water leaves a white crust, rest it overnight and flush the container with clean water once a month. Never allow a saucer to stay full because stagnant water suffocates roots. Resting municipal Cairo tap water for 24 hours in an open barrel allows chlorine to off-gas and heavy sediments to settle at the bottom.

## Sub-Irrigation Planters and Wicking Beds
Sub-irrigation planters (SIPs) provide a bottom water reservoir with a perforated separation screen and capillary soil wick. SIPs reduce evaporation losses by up to 45 percent compared to top watering and provide stable moisture buffer for tomatoes, cucumbers, and eggplants during extreme Cairo heatwaves.

## Salinity Flushing Protocol
High mineral content in local tap water leads to sodium and calcium salt crust accumulation in container root zones. Every 4 weeks, conduct a deep leaching flush by slowly pouring fresh water equal to twice the container volume until runoff flows freely from drainage holes, washing out accumulated salts.
