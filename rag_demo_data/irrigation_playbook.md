# shagara Irrigation Playbook
## Drip schedule
For a 20 litre container, start with 600 to 800 ml every morning. Increase the volume by 20 percent during heatwaves and reduce it after cloudy days. Always check the top 2 cm of soil before repeating a cycle.
## Water quality
If tap water leaves a white crust, rest it overnight and flush the container with clean water once a month. Never allow a saucer to stay full because stagnant water suffocates roots.
