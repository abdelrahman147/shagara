# shagara Pest Field Notes
## Aphids
Aphids cluster on soft new growth. Isolate the pot, rinse the undersides of leaves, then repeat every three days for two weeks. Neem spray should be applied after sunset and tested on one leaf first.
## Powdery mildew
Remove damaged leaves and increase spacing between plants. Water the soil in the morning rather than wetting foliage. Do not compost infected leaves.
