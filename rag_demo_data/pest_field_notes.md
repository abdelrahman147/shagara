# shagara Pest Field Notes
## Aphids
Aphids cluster on soft new growth. Isolate the pot, rinse the undersides of leaves, then repeat every three days for two weeks. Neem spray should be applied after sunset and tested on one leaf first.

## Powdery mildew
Remove damaged leaves and increase spacing between plants. Water the soil in the morning rather than wetting foliage. Do not compost infected leaves. An organic treatment is 5 grams of baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) and 2 drops of vegetable oil in 1 litre of water sprayed at early sunrise.

## Spider Mites
Two-spotted spider mites proliferate rapidly during dry, dusty Cairo heatwaves, causing yellow stippling and fine webbing under foliage. Treat spider mites on mint, tomatoes, and eggplants by rinsing leaf undersides with cold water blasts. Spray an emulsion of 5 ml pure cold-pressed neem oil and 2 ml liquid Castile soap diluted in 1 litre of lukewarm water. Apply thoroughly beneath leaves every 4 days for three consecutive cycles after sunset.

## Whiteflies and Flying Pests
Install bright yellow sticky cards 15 cm above the plant canopy to trap adult whiteflies and thrips. Plant African marigolds and sweet alyssum around tomato containers to attract beneficial predatory hoverflies and parasitic wasps.
