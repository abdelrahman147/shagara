# shagara Rooftop Growing Guide
## Basil in Cairo
Basil performs best with 6 to 8 hours of bright light and airflow around the leaves. Keep the growing medium evenly moist, but let the top 2 cm dry before watering again. Harvest the top leaves weekly to encourage branching.
## Summer heat
During July and August, move containers away from reflective walls and use a 30 percent shade cloth between 11am and 3pm. Morning irrigation reduces evaporation and leaf stress.
