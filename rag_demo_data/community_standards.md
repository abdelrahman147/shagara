# shagara Community Standards
## Harvest sharing
Community beds are harvested on Saturday mornings. Members may take one third of ripe produce and should log the harvest weight in the shared sheet. Always use clean shears disinfected with rubbing alcohol to prevent transferring plant viruses between community beds.

## Compost safety
Only untreated plant scraps are accepted. Meat, dairy, glossy paper, and diseased plants must stay out of the compost bins. Maintain an aerobic compost ratio of 2 parts brown carbon material (dry leaves, shredded cardboard) to 1 part green nitrogen scraps (vegetable trimmings, coffee grounds). Turn compost tumblers twice weekly to accelerate decomposition and maintain internal heat above 55 degrees Celsius.

## Rooftop Structural Safety and Load Limits
Rooftop growers must strictly respect residential structural load limits. Standard Cairo reinforced concrete residential roof slabs safely support a maximum distributed load of 150 to 200 kg per square metre. Heavy planters exceeding 40 litres must always be positioned directly above structural concrete columns or load-bearing perimeter walls, never in the unsupported center of long concrete spans.
